# Recipes

Jobs that cross platforms. Each one imports the API clients and connections from the product folders, such as `providers/google/gmail/` and `providers/microsoft/outlook/`, and can hand off between them with next actions.

| Recipe | What it does |
|---|---|
| [`seo/`](seo/) | **SEO toolkit**: Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and Umami together. A search scorecard, the pages to fix next (with each page's Bing ranking and how long visitors stay), a full report on one page, and which pages Google or Bing haven't indexed |

Next up: receipts and invoices from your inbox into your accounting software.
